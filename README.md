# AURELIA

**An AI-native luxury automotive dealership platform.**

AURELIA is an independent multi-brand luxury automotive dealership that brings the world's most prestigious marques into a single premium digital showroom. Instead of representing one manufacturer, it offers a curated collection of luxury and performance vehicles—including Porsche, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Rolls-Royce, Bentley, McLaren, Aston Martin, Tesla, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and Audi.

The platform combines an elegant luxury experience with an AI-powered concierge that helps customers discover vehicles, compare models, receive personalized recommendations, manage wishlists, and reserve vehicles through a seamless digital journey.

## ✨ Purpose

AURELIA was built to demonstrate how modern AI and full-stack technologies can transform the traditional luxury dealership experience into an intelligent, immersive, and customer-centric platform.
